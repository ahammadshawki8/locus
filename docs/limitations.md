# Limitations

Stated plainly, and kept up to date as gates pass or fail.

- **Automated checks do not certify comprehension.** Passing `touchcheck` means measured spacing, label placement,
  completeness and printer limits are within the profile values. It does not mean a reader will understand the graphic by touch.
- **Rule values come from published guidance**, not from our own testing with readers. See [touch-rules.md](touch-rules.md).
- **No learner outcome data.** We claim time saved and quality checked, not improved learning.
- **Scope:** polynomials up to degree 3 (Tier 1); Grade 1 (uncontracted) braille only; short labels (digits, single letters).
- **Braille is our own minimal table**, not yet checked by a braille transcriber. Cell sizes are the profile's
  (1.5 mm dots, 2.5 mm apart, 6.2 mm between cells, 0.8 mm high), the same as on the calibration piece; how they feel
  is not recorded yet (Gate 7b). Axis labels and tick labels are not on the plate yet.
- **Prints:** the only physical prints are the four prototype plates (calibration, A, B, C), made on 15 and 16 Oct
  before the agent existed. They are "prototype prints", never the agent's printed output.
- **No check that a label sits near its own point.** A label can pass every rule and still be too far from the point
  it names; the overlay and a human look are the check for now.
  No statistical charts, no image-to-tactile, no contracted braille, no printer control.
- **The teacher still handles the physical output** (printing, finishing, checking the print).
- **Status (16 Oct 2026):** nothing is validated with an educator or reader yet. See the status board in
  [projectLocus.md](../projectLocus.md) S.3.
